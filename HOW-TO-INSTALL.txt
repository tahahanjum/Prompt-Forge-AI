PromptForge AI - Android app

1. Copy PromptForge-AI.apk (in this folder) to your phone
   (USB cable, Google Drive, or send it to yourself on WhatsApp/email).
2. On the phone, tap the file. Allow "Install unknown apps" when asked, then tap Install.
   If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap "Install anyway" (it's your own personal build).
3. Open PromptForge AI. No login needed. Everything is stored on your phone.

Optional: More > Settings > AI - paste a Claude API key (console.anthropic.com)
for AI-written scripts in all 10 languages. Without it the app works offline (English).

Back up your work: More > Settings > Your data > Back up.
